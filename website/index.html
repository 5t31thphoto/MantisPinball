How it plays

Left button, right button. That's the whole controller.

Install

Chrome or Edge on a computer · a USB-C data cable · the board plugged in.

  1. Plug the T-Display S3 into your computer.
  2. Click Install and pick the board's serial port from the list.
  3. When it asks about erasing, choose what fits (see the note).
  4. Wait for it to finish — the board restarts into the menu.

About erasing: on a brand-new board, say yes. On a board you already use, say no — that keeps your mantis pet, quests, high scores and WiFi settings.

This browser can't talk to USB devices. Please use Chrome or Edge on a desktop computer. USB access needs a secure (https) page. ⬇ Download .bin
